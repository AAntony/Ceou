Getting started

Add an item and learn how to find it.

What you’ll do

Save your first item and its storage location.

How to do it

  1. In Places, tap Add to create your home.

  2. Open it and add a room. For a car or garage, go straight to storage.

  3. Add storage, such as a cupboard or shelf, and a box if needed.

  4. Add your item. A photo helps you recognise it but is optional.

  5. Search for its name in Find, then open its details.

Tip

Start with things you often look for. The cable drawer can wait.

All set

The item details show each level of its storage path.

Chapter 2 of 11

Finding a belonging

By searching, or by asking out loud.

What you’ll do

Find an item without searching every cupboard.

How to do it

  1. In Find, enter the item’s name. Use Filters to choose a place or room.

  2. Open a result to see its full storage path.

  3. To speak, tap Ask Céoù at the bottom and ask “Where are my scissors?”.

  4. You can also ask to store an item. Check the result and clarify the item or storage if Céoù asks.

  5. Say “That’s all” or close the window when finished. It also closes after 20 seconds without a reply.

Tip

Be specific when items share a name: “the kitchen scissors”, for example.

All set

Find the storage location and open its plan from the item details, if one exists.

Chapter 3 of 11

Adding many items

Scan a photo or a barcode instead of typing everything.

What you’ll do

Add several items from a photo.

How to do it

  1. From a storage location, open the add-item menu and choose Scan a photo.

  2. Take a clear picture of the shelf or open drawer.

  3. Check the suggested items, correct names and uncheck anything to ignore.

  4. Confirm your selection. For a labelled product, you can also scan its barcode.

  5. If the product is unknown, complete its details yourself.

Tip

Recognition can make mistakes. A quick check avoids adding the scenery to your inventory.

All set

Your selected items are added to the storage location.

Chapter 4 of 11

Your receipts and warranties

Keep receipts and track warranties.

What you’ll do

Keep proof of purchase alongside your items.

How to do it

  1. Open an item page and tap “Add a receipt”.

  2. Photograph the till receipt — or import the PDF the shop emailed you.

  3. Note the seller, the purchase date, the amount, and the warranty end date if the item has one.

  4. One receipt can cover several items. Open it from the home’s Receipts screen and add them: each keeps its own amount and warranty.

  5. From another item page, choose “One already saved” to attach it to the same receipt without photographing the paper again.

  6. The “Without receipt” tab lists everything with no proof yet. The export button, at the top, builds the complete file.

Tip

Add the warranty end date to receive the reminder scheduled 30 days before it expires.

All set

Export a PDF of your supporting documents from the home’s invoices.

Chapter 5 of 11

Your home’s floor plan

Explore rooms and locate your items.

What you’ll do

See which room and storage to look in.

How to do it

  1. In Places, open your home, then Plans. Open a plan or add one; a template can help you get started.

  2. Tap a room to zoom in. Its numbered storage locations appear in the list below.

  3. Tap Edit to draw or move rooms and storage, then Done when finished.

  4. From an item’s details, tap View on plan. Its room is framed and placed storage gets a larger numbered marker.

  5. The card below shows the item and its path. Close it to explore other rooms.

Tip

If storage is not placed on the plan, Céoù highlights the room. List view also lets you read the plan without using the drawing.

All set

The same number connects the storage marker to your item’s card.

Chapter 6 of 11

Plan a move

Find your things among the boxes.

What you’ll do

Track boxes from packing to unpacking.

How to do it

  1. In Places, open Organise a move and create your move.

  2. Choose the starting place and destination.

  3. Create a box, add a photo and pack your items into it.

  4. Use the actions to scan a box or print labels. Follow progress at the top of the screen.

  5. On arrival, switch to unpacking and choose where to put each item.

Tip

Your move stays private until you choose Share with friends. Permissions follow the homes involved.

All set

Each box keeps its list of items. Handy when you need that charger on the first night.

Chapter 7 of 11

Lending and borrowing

Remember what left your home, and what you owe back.

What you’ll do

Remember who has your things and what you need to return.

How to do it

  1. On an item page, tap “Lend or borrow”.

  2. Say which way it goes: you are lending it, or you borrowed it.

  3. Pick a friend from the list, or simply type a name — the person does not need to have Céoù.

  4. Set a return date if you have one: Céoù will remind you on the day. Without a date, the loan stays in the list until you close it.

  5. When the item comes back, a button on its page or in the Loans screen closes the loan.

Tip

The drill hasn’t vanished. Its details tell you who borrowed it.

All set

Loans stay available until you close them.

Chapter 8 of 11

Sharing with people close to you

Give access to a home, and choose how far it goes.

What you’ll do

Share an inventory with people you know.

How to do it

  1. In Profile, open Friends and invitations to find your friend code.

  2. Your friend enters it or scans its QR from Sharing, then Add a friend.

  3. Accept the request. Becoming friends does not share any places.

  4. Open their details and choose permissions for each home: view, edit or owner.

  5. Change these permissions whenever needed.

Tip

Permissions apply to each place. Sharing the house doesn’t mean sharing the garage.

All set

Your friend can access only the places you shared.

Chapter 9 of 11

Access with no account

A read-only code for a tenant, a tradesperson, a house-sitter.

What you’ll do

Give someone view-only access without creating an account.

How to do it

  1. In Profile → Friends and invitations, tap Generate an access code.

  2. Choose the homes they can view.

  3. Set the duration and number of uses.

  4. Name the code so you can recognise it, for example September guests.

  5. Share the link or code. Your guest chooses I have an invitation code to enter.

Tip

Manage or revoke access under My invitation codes in the same section.

All set

Guests can view authorised places without changing the inventory.

Chapter 10 of 11

Adjusting the display

Theme, text size, language.

What you’ll do

Choose a display that feels comfortable.

How to do it

  1. In Profile, open My preferences.

  2. Choose Français or English for the language.

  3. Turn dark mode on or off, or let Céoù follow your phone.

  4. Choose a text size and check the preview. It applies straight away.

Tip

Your phone’s text size settings are also taken into account.

All set

Text and controls adapt throughout the app.

Chapter 11 of 11

With no network

What Céoù can do in a cellar or a garage.

What you’ll do

Keep organising when the signal drops.

How to do it

  1. Open Céoù while connected before you leave: available data is kept on your phone.

  2. Offline, browse your inventory and add, rename or move items.

  3. Pending changes are sent when the connection returns.

  4. If a change is rejected, use the banner to review it or try again.

Tip

Voice, photo recognition, barcode product lookup and invoice export need a connection.

All set

Local changes wait for a connection before syncing.
